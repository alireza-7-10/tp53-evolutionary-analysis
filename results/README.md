# Results

This folder contains the output files of the TP53 evolutionary analysis.

## Contents

### Figures
- **Figure1_Phylogenetic_Tree.png**: Maximum Likelihood phylogenetic tree of TP53 CDS across seven vertebrate species (Tamura-Nei model, 1000 bootstrap replicates). *Danio rerio* was used as the outgroup.
- **Figure2_FEL_Plot.png**: Plot of estimated synonymous (α) and nonsynonymous (β) substitution rates at codon sites of the TP53 gene, based on Fixed Effects Likelihood (FEL) analysis on the Datamonkey server.

### Data Tables
- **genetic_distance_matrix.csv**: Pairwise genetic distance matrix calculated based on the Tamura-Nei (TN93) model in MEGA 12.
- **fel_selection_sites.csv**: List of codon sites identified as being under significant purifying or diversifying selection (p ≤ 0.05) by the FEL method.

## Summary of Key Findings
- Lowest genetic distance: *Homo sapiens* vs. *Macaca mulatta* (3.66%)
- Highest genetic distance: *Danio rerio* vs. mammals (52.7% – 57.6%)
- Total significant selection sites: 16 out of 389 non-invariant codons
  - 12 sites under purifying selection
  - 4 sites under diversifying/positive selection (codons 14, 174, 318, 380)
